# Decision Support Scope & Limitations

## Explicit Out-of-Scope Items (Module 09)
The calculated figures represent **Estimated Net Realization** after explicitly entered selling costs. They do **NOT** guarantee farm profit or income.

Specifically, Module 09 does **NOT** account for:
- Seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and soil amendments
- Labor, machinery depreciation, fuel, and irrigation costs
- Land rent, financing, or interest costs
- Spoilage, shrinkage, or quality deductions
- Mandi commission or unentered handling fees
- Traffic congestion, waiting time, or transit delays
- Buyer reliability or risk of non-payment
- Future price fluctuations or market volatility
- AI/ML predictions or forecasting

All results are deterministic estimates based on verified past observation data and farmer inputs.
