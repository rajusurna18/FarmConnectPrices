# Module 10 — Limitations & Scope Boundaries

## Model Scope Boundaries
Module 10 is a **deterministic farm economic & profitability foundation**. It explicitly does **NOT** constitute complete financial accounting or AI prediction.

## Excluded Capabilities (By Design)
1. **No AI / Machine Learning**: No natural-language selling advice, price prediction, or yield forecasting models.
2. **No Uncaptured Implicit Costs**: Does not automatically account for:
   - Unpaid family labor opportunity cost
   - Equipment depreciation schedule
   - Land opportunity cost
   - Credit / loan interest rates
   - Post-harvest spoilage / quality deductions
   - Mandi commission variations
3. **No Marketplace / Transaction Execution**: No offer listings, order placement, payments, or logistics transport booking.
4. **No Financial Loan / Tax Systems**: Not accounting or GST software.

## Estimates Labeling Standard
All profitability outputs (`estimatedProfit`, `breakEvenSellingPrice`, `roi`, `profitabilityStatus`) are labeled as **Estimates** in UI and API payloads to prevent misleading farmers about guaranteed profits.
